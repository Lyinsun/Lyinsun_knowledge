# Ingest

Confirm authorization and privacy boundaries first. Stage unverified material in `00-Inbox/`; identify source, retrieval date, and verification gaps. Compile only the necessary notes into projects, resources, or knowledge cards, and link the derived notes back to a concise source record. Never import credentials, personal data, or restricted raw text just for completeness.
